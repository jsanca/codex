SUMMARY
-------
Introduced RoleAssignment as the final Phase 1 structural primitive. It is a three-field
record: Actor + RoleKey + ResourceScope. It carries no Role blueprint, no PermissionKey,
no evaluation logic, and no agent-type restrictions. Those belong in the future
PermissionResolver and policy engine.

OBSERVATIONS
------------
- RoleAssignment holds RoleKey, not Role. This is intentional: the assignment records
  which role an actor holds; resolving what permissions that role grants requires looking
  up the Role blueprint, which belongs in PermissionResolver. Embedding the full Role
  here would couple assignment storage to permission definitions.

- isSameAs used for actor preservation — records store component references, so the same
  Actor instance should come back identical. Consistent with how PermissionGrantTest
  asserts isSameAs on Permissions constants.

- Structural guards (no PermissionKey field, no Role field, no evaluate/isGranted method)
  use getDeclaredFields/getDeclaredMethods as in prior tasks.
