Introduced PermissionGrant as the next Phase 1 primitive. No other types were added.
PermissionGrant is a two-field record: PermissionKey + ResourceScope. It carries no actor,
no role, no implication logic, no site-boundary resolution, and no persistence metadata.

OBSERVATIONS
------------
- PermissionGrant is intentionally minimal. It is a data record, not a decision. The
  JavaDoc states this explicitly and names PermissionResolver as the future home for
  implication and resolution logic.

- Structural tests (no Actor field, no Role field, no evaluate/isGranted method) use
  getDeclaredFields() and getDeclaredMethods() as guards against accidental scope creep
  in future tasks. Same technique used in RoleTest and PermissionsTest.

- isSameAs used for Permissions constants in the preservesPermission test — record
  components are stored by reference, so the same static constant should come back
  identical. This is a slightly stronger assertion than isEqualTo and documents the
  expected identity behavior.
